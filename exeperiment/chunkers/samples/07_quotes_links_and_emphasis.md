# Editorial review notes

## An attributed observation

> The reader should be able to distinguish what was observed from what was
> inferred. A short explanation of the inference is more useful than an
> unexplained statement of confidence.
>
> Keep the qualification next to the claim when the qualification changes
> how the claim should be interpreted.

The quotation is fictional. It provides a multi-paragraph blockquote for
inspection rather than reproducing an external publication.

## A review reminder

> [!NOTE]
> An incomplete attachment list is a request for follow-up, not a rejection of
> the entire submission.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not publish a draft decision as though it were the completed review.

## Inline context

Use *emphasis* to call attention to a distinction and **strong emphasis** for
a decision that the reader must not overlook. An identifier such as `DOC-301`
should remain recognizable even when it appears in the middle of a sentence.
The field name `review_state` is different from the display label "Review state".

The [fictional service guide](https://example.invalid/service-guide) and the
[review glossary][glossary] represent two different link styles. They are
placeholder links and do not need to be opened to use this sample.

## A nested quotation

> The reviewer asked for a more precise claim.
>
> > The author responded with the observation that supported the claim and the
> > condition under which that observation was collected.
>
> The resulting discussion belongs in the review history.
