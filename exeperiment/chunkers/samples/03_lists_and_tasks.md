# Document intake checklist

## Required information

* Document identity
  * A stable external identifier
  * A descriptive title
  * The revision date supplied by the author
* Submission context
  * The submitting team
  * The reason for the review
  * The collection that should receive the completed document
* Supporting material
  * A list of related documents
  * An explanation of any missing attachments

## Intake sequence

1. Open the submission and confirm that it can be read.

   If the submission is damaged, record the failure before asking for a
   replacement. A replacement should not erase the original submission record.

2. Compare the supplied identifier with existing records.

   A matching identifier may indicate a revision rather than a duplicate.
   Compare the revision date and the submitting team before deciding.

3. Assign an owner and record the next review action.

   The owner coordinates the review but does not need to perform every task.

4. Notify the submitting team that intake is complete.

## Completion criteria

An unchecked task is not an approval. The final reviewer records a decision
only after the required evidence is available.
